Meet Your Doctor

Healing Hearts, Enriching Lives

"A heart is not just a pump; it holds the dreams, smiles, and futures of a family. Every procedure we perform is a trust we must honor."

Growing up in Rajasthan, Dr. Arjun Mehta witnessed the gap in timely cardiac interventions in non-metropolitan regions. Determined to bridge this, he dedicated himself to medical excellence, securing his medical qualifications and senior specialization from the prestigious AIIMS New Delhi.

After completing his DM in Cardiology at SMS Medical College, Jaipur, and a coveted Fellowship in Interventional Cardiology at Mount Sinai Hospital, New York, he returned to his home state. He established HeartCare Advanced Cardiac Centre on Tonk Road, right next to SMS Hospital, bringing global interventional standards closer to the citizens of Rajasthan.

Dr. Mehta specializes in radial approach angiography and angioplasty—a patient-preferred technique that minimizes bleeding, eliminates the need for groin cuts, and enables same-day discharge.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have compiled responses to the most common queries patients have regarding heart procedures, insurance panel approvals, and clinic consultations.

An interventional cardiologist (like Dr. Arjun Mehta) specializes in treating cardiovascular diseases using minimally invasive, catheter-based procedures (like radial angioplasty and stenting) through small punctures in the wrist or leg, avoiding open incisions. A cardiothoracic surgeon, on the other hand, performs open heart surgeries such as coronary artery bypass grafts (CABG) or heart transplants.
You should consult Dr. Mehta directly if you experience symptoms like chest discomfort, pressure, or shortness of breath, unexplained palpitations, dizziness, or fainting spells. Patients with high-risk factors like chronic diabetes, long-standing hypertension, high cholesterol, or a strong family history of heart disease should also undergo regular cardiovascular screenings.
We provide full diagnostic screenings in-house including 12-lead ECG, high-resolution 3D Echocardiography, Treadmill Stress Testing (TMT), and 24/48-hour Holter ambulatory rhythm monitoring. In most instances, ECG and Echocardiography results are parsed, written, and handed over to you immediately during your consultation session.
Radial angioplasty is a procedure where catheters are routed to the heart through the radial artery in your wrist rather than the femoral artery in your groin. Key benefits include a significant reduction in bleeding risks, elimination of the need to lie flat on your back for hours after the procedure, immediate post-procedure mobility, and overall faster recovery, enabling discharge within 24 to 48 hours.
For elective radial angioplasty, patients are usually discharged within 48 hours. You can resume light, routine indoor activities within 3 to 5 days. We advise avoiding heavy lifting, strenuous workouts, and driving for approximately 1 to 2 weeks. A structured cardiorespiratory recovery plan and detailed lifestyle guidelines will be customized for you by Dr. Mehta upon discharge.
