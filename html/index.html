Meet Your Doctor

Healing Hearts, Enriching Lives

Growing up in Rajasthan, Dr. Arjun Mehta witnessed the gap in timely cardiac interventions in non-metropolitan regions. Determined to bridge this, he dedicated himself to medical excellence, securing his qualifications from the prestigious AIIMS New Delhi.

With advanced fellowship training at Mount Sinai Hospital, New York, one of America's top cardiac centres, Dr. Mehta brings global expertise to Jaipur. His patient-first philosophy and mastery of minimally invasive techniques ensure world-class outcomes for every patient.

Today, he leads HeartCare Advanced Cardiac Centre — a state-of-the-art facility offering comprehensive cardiac care under one roof, from advanced diagnostics to complex interventional procedures.

Patient Education

Frequently Asked Questions

An interventional cardiologist performs catheter-based procedures (like angioplasty and stenting) without open surgery. A heart surgeon performs coronary artery bypass grafting (CABG) and other open-heart procedures. Dr. Mehta specialises in minimally invasive interventions with faster recovery times.

If you experience chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations, dizziness, or have risk factors like diabetes, hypertension, or a family history of heart disease, a cardiology consultation is recommended. Dr. Mehta provides comprehensive evaluation and personalised treatment plans.

Our centre offers in-house 3D echocardiography, stress tests, Holter monitoring, ECG, and blood work. Most results are available within 30-60 minutes, allowing same-day consultation and diagnosis.

Radial angioplasty is performed through the wrist artery rather than the groin. Benefits include immediate walking, lower bleeding risk, no groin pain, and faster discharge — often the same day. Dr. Mehta is a pioneer of this technique in Jaipur.

Most patients resume normal activities within 2-3 days. We recommend avoiding heavy lifting for 1 week. Complete recovery with lifestyle modifications is guided through our cardiac rehabilitation program.

Yes. We are empanelled with RGHS, CGHS, and all major private insurance providers. Our team handles the paperwork to ensure a cashless and hassle-free experience for our patients.
